ℹ️ How are tiers determined? (click to expand)

Tier boundaries are vendor-neutral and mathematically derived:

  • Budget Tiers: Log-spaced across SF's hourly rate range (since Athena is serverless with a fixed cost). At each tier, SF's best config competes against Athena's single price point.
  • SLA Tiers: Log-spaced from just above the fastest config to the slowest. Tight SLAs expose capability gaps where Athena can't compete at all.

Why this is fair:

  • Tier boundaries come from mathematical distribution, not either vendor's specific config values
  • Log-scale spacing is natural for both cost and latency metrics
  • Each tier finds the best qualifying config from SF independently
  • Athena's serverless model means it appears identically in every price tier — a fair reflection of its fixed-cost model
